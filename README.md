# UK Dog Anxiety Resources

Free guides, tools and organisations for UK owners of anxious dogs. The list covers separation anxiety, fear of fireworks and other noises, car travel, rescue dogs settling in, and the calming products, supplements and prescription medicines available in the UK.

Each entry gives specific information an owner can act on: doses by weight, running costs, week-by-week plans, or who to contact for professional help. Many of the guides and calculators come from [Captain Calm](https://captaincalm.co.uk), a UK site that publishes dosing charts and product comparisons for anxious dogs. The rest are from UK animal welfare charities and professional bodies.

## Recognising anxiety

- [Does my dog have anxiety? Quiz](https://captaincalm.co.uk/tools/anxiety-quiz) - Ten questions that check for separation anxiety, noise phobia and general stress.
- [Signs of anxiety in dogs](https://captaincalm.co.uk/guides/dog-anxiety-symptoms) - 15 physical and behavioural signs, from lip licking to destructive behaviour, and what each one means.

## Separation anxiety

- [Separation anxiety assessment](https://captaincalm.co.uk/tools/separation-anxiety-assessment) - A quiz that estimates how severe the problem is and gives a training timeline.
- [Dog separation anxiety: UK guide](https://captaincalm.co.uk/guides/dog-separation-anxiety) - Symptoms, causes, treatment and training.
- [RSPCA: separation anxiety in dogs](https://www.rspca.org.uk/adviceandwelfare/pets/dogs/behaviour/separationrelatedbehaviour) - RSPCA advice for dogs that struggle when left alone.

- [PDSA: separation anxiety in dogs](https://www.pdsa.org.uk/pet-help-and-advice/pet-health-hub/other-veterinary-advice/separation-anxiety-in-dogs) - Symptoms, treatment and prevention from PDSA vets.

## Car travel

- [Dog anxiety in the car](https://captaincalm.co.uk/guides/car-anxiety-dogs) - Eight symptoms, including panting, drooling and shaking, with a desensitisation plan and the signs that sudden car anxiety needs a vet rather than training.

## Rescue dogs

## Calming products

- [Calming treats for dogs that actually work](https://captaincalm.co.uk/guides/calming-treats-evidence-based-review) - Which active ingredients have evidence in dogs (L-theanine, alpha-casozepine) and which mostly do not (valerian, chamomile), with onset times and doses by weight.
- [UK calming chews compared](https://captaincalm.co.uk/guides/calming-chews-for-dogs-uk) - Nutracalm, ADAPTIL Chew, VETIQ Serene and NutriPaw compared on dose, onset, evidence and monthly cost for a 20 kg dog.
- [Best calming tablets for dogs in the UK](https://captaincalm.co.uk/reviews/best-calming-supplements-dogs-uk) - Zylkene, YuMOVE, Nutracalm, Anxitane and Dorwest Scullcap & Valerian compared by ingredient and format.
- [Plug-in calming diffusers compared](https://captaincalm.co.uk/reviews/best-pheromone-diffusers-uk) - Adaptil, Pet Remedy and Beaphar diffusers, with evidence ratings and yearly running costs.
- [Adaptil vs Pet Remedy](https://captaincalm.co.uk/guides/adaptil-vs-pet-remedy) - A pheromone diffuser against a valerian-based one: evidence, cost per year, and which suits which situation.
- [Calming dog beds tested](https://captaincalm.co.uk/reviews/best-calming-beds-uk) - Four beds compared, and the cases where a calming bed will not help.
- [All calming options compared](https://captaincalm.co.uk/guides/natural-calming-solutions) - Supplements, pheromones, sprays, anxiety vests, beds and medication side by side, matched to the type of anxiety.

- [Dog anxiety medication in the UK](https://captaincalm.co.uk/guides/dog-anxiety-medication-uk-guide) - The prescription medicines UK vets use for anxiety, including fluoxetine, trazodone, clomipramine and gabapentin: typical doses, how long each takes to work, side effects, and when over-the-counter tablets are enough.

## Finding a vet or behaviourist

A vet should rule out pain or illness before a behaviour plan starts.

- [RCVS Find a Vet](https://findavet.rcvs.org.uk/) - Search the Royal College of Veterinary Surgeons register for practices near you.
- [Animal Behaviour and Training Council](https://abtc.org.uk/) - Sets the UK standards for animal behaviourists and trainers and keeps registers of those who meet them.
- [Association of Pet Behaviour Counsellors](https://www.apbc.org.uk/) - UK pet behaviour counsellors who work on referral from vets.
